# MEMORYMINT

## Instant Memorial Platform + Digital Guestbook + Memorial Card Ecosystem

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

MemoryMint is NO LONGER just a memorial QR card product.

MemoryMint is now:

> An instant memorial experience platform that allows grieving families to create a beautiful digital memorial in minutes, collect stories/photos/videos from guests during funeral services, and later preserve those memories permanently using stainless steel MemoryMint cards.

The stainless steel card is now:

- the physical extension of the memorial
- the permanent QR gateway
- the symbolic keepsake

The TRUE core product is:

- the instant memorial experience
- memorial guestbook collection
- emotional memory preservation
